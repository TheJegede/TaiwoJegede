# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, technical interviewers, collaborators, and potential clients reviewing Taiwo Jegede's work quickly before deciding whether to contact him. They need a credible snapshot of his AI, data, cloud, and software engineering experience, with enough concrete project evidence to trust the claims.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Taiwo as a sharp, practical AI and data professional who can connect machine learning, analytics, cloud deployment, and user-facing software. Success means visitors understand his strongest capabilities, remember the projects that prove them, and have a clear path to contact him.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, credible, warm. The voice should feel confident and human, using specific outcomes, technologies, and product context instead of vague superlatives.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-template language, identical filler cards, empty hype, recruiter buzzword padding, and visual changes that make the page feel less personal or harder to scan.

## Design Principles

- Show proof before polish: project cards should make the real work, stack, and impact easy to understand.
- Keep recruiter scanning fast: write concise descriptions with strong verbs and concrete details.
- Let personality stay present: maintain warmth and momentum without slipping into casual clutter.
- Preserve the current identity: changes should fit the existing dark, glassy portfolio rather than resetting the visual system.
- Prefer honest specificity: do not invent metrics, deployment status, links, or capabilities that are not grounded in the codebase or provided context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, keyboard-accessible links and controls, descriptive link labels, reduced-motion compatibility where motion is added, and responsive layouts that keep text legible on mobile.
